GKT // Find Your 7 Twins

Privacy & consent

This application compares facial images for visual similarity.

What the service does

Find Your 7 Twins generates a facial embedding from the single-face photo you submit and compares it with embeddings from discoverable profiles. The result is a visual-similarity ranking.

It does not determine genetic relationships, ancestry, identity, or biological twin status.

What we store

  • Your account information, including username, email, name and country.
  • Your uploaded profile photo and its generated visual embedding while the account exists.
  • Your discovery decisions and mutual matches.
  • Short-lived password-reset tokens in hashed form when password recovery is requested.

Control and deletion

You can turn discoverability off from your profile. You can permanently delete your account from the profile page; account data and the associated uploaded photo are deleted by the application.

Password recovery

Reset links expire after a limited period and are single-use. In production, reset links are sent through the configured email provider and are not returned in normal API responses.

Questions

For questions about the application, use the contact address published on the main GKT website.
